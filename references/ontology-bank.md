# Ontology Bank for Anti-Collapse Divergence

Use this file when the user wants unusually broad brainstorming. Do not use all ontologies every time. Pick the smallest set that creates real spread.

For each ontology, avoid the cliché mapping unless you mutate it into a new mechanism.

| Ontology | Cliché mapping to avoid | Better search questions |
|---|---|---|
| Status / hierarchy | badges, prestige, exclusivity | What costly signal changes rank? Who imitates whom? What absence becomes embarrassing? |
| Religion / ritual | generic weekly ritual | What becomes profane to skip? What crossing requires blessing, confession, or sacrifice? |
| Epidemiology | virality | What is the vector, host, symptom, immunity, mutation, or reproduction number? |
| Supply chain | pipeline metaphor | Who rejects defective inputs? What upstream certification becomes mandatory? |
| Political movement | activism slogans | What grievance creates coalition? Who is the enemy? What power is redistributed? |
| Games | leaderboard / points | What converts luck to skill? Where is PvP, progression, unlock, boss fight, or exploit? |
| Dating / marketplaces | matching | What makes quality legible before commitment? What creates adverse selection or liquidity? |
| Piracy / contraband | illegal sharing | What forbidden use creates backchannel pull? What travels because it is mildly transgressive? |
| Education / apprenticeship | courses | What habit is imprinted during formation? What vocabulary do graduates carry elsewhere? |
| Luxury / scarcity | premium pricing | What desire is created by refusal, gatekeeping, provenance, or visible restraint? |
| Open-source / commons | free community | What standard is captured by gifting vocabulary, IDs, protocols, or primitives? |
| Insurance / risk | risk reduction | Who bears downside? What audit trail reduces blame or premiums? |
| Comedy / memes | funny posts | What output format is inherently remixable? What repeated template makes the idea mutate? |
| Labor unions / guilds | worker activism | What craft standard protects practitioners from arbitrary override? |
| Military doctrine | command-and-control | What becomes doctrine, red-team loop, after-action review, or rules of engagement? |
| Hospitality | white-glove service | What private embarrassment is prevented? How does the product behave like a discreet host? |
| Urban geography | maps | Where is the natural desire path, bottleneck, zoning gate, district, or crossroads? |
| Addiction / habit loops | dopamine | What itch, cue, variable reward, relief loop, or withdrawal forms? |
| Trust / security | badges / encryption | What zero-trust handshake, attestation, threat model, or attack vector changes behavior? |
| Bureaucracy / compliance | checkbox | What form field gates progress? What process becomes ownerless and hard to remove? |
| Ecology | ecosystem | What niche, predator, parasite, symbiosis, invasive species, or succession dynamic exists? |
| Agriculture | growth metaphor | What is seeded, irrigated, harvested, composted, or rotated? What needs seasons? |
| Physics | gravity / momentum | What force, friction, phase transition, resonance, or conservation law governs adoption? |
| Chemistry | reactions | What catalyst lowers activation energy? What reagent is missing? What produces toxicity? |
| Biology / evolution | survival | What selection pressure, mutation, camouflage, mimicry, or reproductive advantage appears? |
| Medicine / triage | diagnosis | What gets screened, quarantined, escalated, or treated before symptoms become severe? |
| Law / courts | compliance | What evidence, precedent, burden of proof, appeal, or adversarial hearing changes decisions? |
| Forensics | investigation | What trace evidence, chain of custody, reconstruction, or latent fingerprint reveals truth? |
| Finance / options | ROI | What optionality, hedge, leverage, arbitrage, insurance, or portfolio logic applies? |
| Navigation / maritime | journey | What lighthouse, port, current, map, storm, convoy, or hidden reef shapes movement? |
| Theater / performance | storytelling | What backstage/frontstage split, rehearsal, role, audience, or prop creates behavior? |
| Music | rhythm | What cadence, tension/release, motif, harmony, improvisation, or audience participation matters? |
| Fashion | trends | What becomes a look, uniform, taboo, season, knockoff, or identity signal? |
| Sports | competition | What training loop, scouting report, playbook, unfair advantage, or referee changes incentives? |
| Diplomacy / negotiation | partnership | What treaty, hostage, face-saving mechanism, sanction, or backchannel unlocks movement? |
| Manufacturing / quality | automation | What inspection gate, tolerance, scrap, rework, or statistical control prevents bad outputs? |
| Software compiler | code metaphor | What parses, type-checks, optimizes, or rejects invalid inputs before runtime? |
| Operating system | platform | What background daemon, scheduler, permission layer, or interrupt handler changes flow? |
| Mythology | hero story | What archetype, forbidden knowledge, oracle, trickster, curse, or initiation creates pull? |
| Psychology / therapy | self-help | What defense mechanism, shadow, exposure loop, reframing, or attachment pattern is active? |
| Logistics | delivery | What route, hub, last-mile constraint, inventory buffer, or SLA creates adoption pressure? |
| Architecture | building | What load-bearing wall, threshold, facade, room, scaffold, or fire escape shapes behavior? |
| Cartography | maps | What boundary, projection distortion, blank space, or legend changes what people can see? |
| Intelligence / espionage | spying | What signal, source, cover, counterintelligence, or leak creates advantage? |
| Religion heresy | ritual cliché | What doctrine is challenged? What heresy spreads because orthodoxy fails? |
| Migration | travel | What crossing, visa, diaspora, remittance, or bridge population carries the idea? |
| Festivals / carnivals | events | What temporary inversion of norms lets people try the forbidden or strange safely? |
